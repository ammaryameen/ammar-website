About me

I finished my intermediate in 2024 and I’m now doing a bachelor’s degree in computer science. Alongside my studies, I work part-time as a content creator and photographer, and I’ve been doing it for around four to five years.

I work with brands and make videos for my YouTube channel. I shoot my videos and reels on my own phone. For client photography projects, the brand provides the camera and other gear, so I’ve learned to get good results from whatever equipment is in front of me.

Photography and content creation are how I express myself, so I see my work as art as much as a job. Fashion and sneakers are part of the same creative side.

I try to learn as much as I can, every day. That’s why I’m now working through my web development journey.
